PRICING MODEL:
Freshdesk offers per-agent subscription plans plus consumption packs for Freddy AI:
- Free Tier: Up to 10 agents with basic email ticketing (no AI).
- Growth Tier: $15 per agent/month (billed annually). Includes basic automations and standard marketplace apps.
- Pro Tier: $49 per agent/month (billed annually). Includes round-robin routing, SLA management, and multilingual knowledge base.
- Enterprise Tier: $79 per agent/month (billed annually). Advanced skill-based routing and sandbox.

FREDDY AI ADD-ON & SESSIONS:
- Freddy Copilot: $29 per agent/month add-on fee for Pro and Enterprise tiers.
- Freddy Self-Service Sessions: 500 free Freddy Self-Service bot sessions are included with Pro and Enterprise plans.
- Additional Bot Sessions: Sold in packs of 1,000 sessions for $100 (equating to $0.10 per bot conversation session).
