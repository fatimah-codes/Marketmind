PLATFORM OVERVIEW:
Intercom provides an AI-first customer service platform designed around the Fin AI engine. The platform consists of:
1. Fin AI Agent: An autonomous bot capable of answering complex inquiries directly from synced knowledge base content, public URLs, and internal docs.
2. Fin AI Copilot: An in-inbox assistant for human agents that suggests replies, summarizes multi-turn conversations, and composes outbound updates.
3. Omnichannel Messenger: In-app chat, mobile SDK, email, and social integrations (WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger).
