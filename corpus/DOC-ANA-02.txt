TITLE: Forrester Wave: Conversational AI For Customer Service
VENDOR: Industry Analyst
SOURCE TYPE: analyst_report
PUBLISHED: 2025-12-18
TARGET ENTITY: Market Overview

KEY CRITERIA:
Platforms were evaluated on RAG integration fidelity, guardrails against hallucination, agent handoff transparency, and total cost predictability.

NOTABLE FINDINGS:
- Architecture Shift: Over 72% of surveyed enterprise contact centers are shifting from rigid dialog flows to hybrid LLM reasoning agents grounded in internal knowledge bases.
- Vendor Differentiation: Pure-play AI engines (Ada, Forethought) deliver higher initial deflection rates (35-45%), while platform suites (Salesforce, Zendesk, Freshworks) provide lower integration friction and consolidated audit logging.
- Buyer Pain Points: 64% of procurement leaders cite unpredictable consumption charges (per-resolution or per-credit pricing) as the primary inhibitor to scaling autonomous AI agents.
