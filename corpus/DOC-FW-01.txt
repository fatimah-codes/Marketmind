TITLE: Freshworks Freshdesk and Freddy AI Copilot Overview
VENDOR: Freshworks
SOURCE TYPE: vendor_doc
PUBLISHED: 2025-12-01
TARGET ENTITY: Freshworks Freddy AI

OVERVIEW:
Freshworks integrates its generative Freddy AI across Freshdesk (ticketing) and Freshchat (conversational messaging).
Freddy AI is offered in three modalities:
1. Freddy Self-Service: Customer-facing bots for autonomous issue resolution on web and WhatsApp.
2. Freddy Copilot: In-line agent assistant for text rephrasing, response generation, and automated ticket summary.
3. Freddy Insights: Manager-level dashboard surfacing operational bottlenecks, churn risks, and agent coaching suggestions.
