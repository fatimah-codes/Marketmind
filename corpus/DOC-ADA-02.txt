PRICING MODEL:
Ada operates primarily on an outcome-based and volume-tiered resolution model rather than traditional agent seat licenses:
- Starter Package: $30,000 annual baseline platform fee, including up to 25,000 automated resolutions annually (~$1.20 per resolution effective).
- Growth Package: $60,000 annual platform fee, including 60,000 automated resolutions (~$1.00 per resolution effective).
- Enterprise Custom: Custom quotation with dedicated customer success manager, custom LLM fine-tuning, and volume discounts dropping resolution costs to $0.70-$0.85 per resolution at >200,000 annual volumes.
- Channel Surcharges: Native Voice AI resolution carries a $0.20 per-minute telephony carrier surcharge.
