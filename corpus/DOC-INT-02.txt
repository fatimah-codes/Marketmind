COMMERCIAL MODEL AND PRICING:
Intercom uses a core seat price combined with a consumption-based pricing model for Fin AI:
- Essential Tier: $39 per seat/month (billed annually). Geared toward early-stage companies and smaller support desks.
- Advanced Tier: $99 per seat/month. Includes custom workflows, round-robin assignment, and multilingual support.
- Expert Tier: $139 per seat/month. Advanced permissions, SSO, and real-time workload management.

FIN AI RESOLUTION PRICING & PERFORMANCE:
- Cost per Resolution: Intercom charges a flat fee of $0.99 per successful automated resolution. A resolution is counted only when the customer explicitly confirms the issue was solved or ceases the conversation without escalating to a human agent.
- Claimed Resolution Rate: Intercom official documentation asserts that Fin AI achieves a verified 50% automated resolution rate across inbound customer queries without human intervention.
- Copilot Add-On: Fin AI Copilot for human reps is priced at $35 per agent/month.
