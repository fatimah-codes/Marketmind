GOVERNANCE AUDIT:
Enterprise deployment of autonomous LLM support agents requires strict adherence to privacy and security boundaries:
- PII Masking: Automated redaction of credit card numbers (PCI-DSS), Social Security numbers, and health records prior to sending context to third-party model providers.
- Indirect Prompt Injection Vulnerability: Research indicates that 43% of unhardened support bots can be manipulated if malicious instructions are embedded within indexed user support documentation, web pages, or customer email signatures.
- Recommended Defenses:
  1. Strict structural separation between system instructions and retrieved customer data.
  2. Provenance tagging of all retrieved text fragments.
  3. Pre-execution scanning for prompt injection trigger words ("SYSTEM: ignore prior instructions", "DAN mode", "jailbreak").
