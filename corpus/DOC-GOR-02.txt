COMMERCIAL PRICING TIERS:
Gorgias charges by ticket volume rather than individual agent seats:
- Starter: $10/month (billed monthly) or $90/year. Includes 50 billable tickets/month and 3 user seats.
- Basic: $60/month (billed monthly) or $50/month (annual). Includes 300 tickets/month, unlimited seats, and live chat.
- Pro: $360/month (billed monthly) or $300/month (annual). Includes 2,000 tickets/month and user roles.
- Advanced: $900/month (billed monthly) or $750/month (annual). Includes 5,000 tickets/month and dedicated support.

AUTOMATE AI ADD-ON:
- Priced at $1.00 per automated interaction.
- E-commerce self-service flows included.

ENTERPRISE CUSTOM TIERS:
- For organizations exceeding 10,000 tickets/month, custom high-volume ticket blocks are available.
[Note: Gorgias does not disclose public pricing for custom LLM model fine-tuning or contractual Enterprise SLA uptime percentages in published documentation.]
