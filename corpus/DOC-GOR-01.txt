PLATFORM FOCUS:
Gorgias is an all-in-one helpdesk and AI customer service platform built specifically for e-commerce brands on Shopify, BigCommerce, and Magento:
- Deep Shopify Integration: Allows agents and AI bots to view order details, issue refunds, adjust shipping addresses, and duplicate orders directly within the chat interface.
- Automate AI Add-on: AI-driven answers for top e-commerce inquiries ("Where is my order?", "Can I change my delivery date?").
- Pre-built Macros & Rules: Over 50 e-commerce specific automated rules and intents.
