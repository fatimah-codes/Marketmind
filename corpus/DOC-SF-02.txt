COMMERCIAL TIERS & AGENTFORCE PRICING:
Salesforce positions at the upper enterprise tier with seat licenses and consumption credits:
- Enterprise Edition: $165 per user/month (annual billing). Full custom CRM objects, API integrations, and workflow rules.
- Unlimited Edition: $330 per user/month. Includes premier support, built-in Einstein AI features (summaries, reply recommendation).
- Einstein 1 Service Edition: $500 per user/month. Bundles full Service Cloud Unlimited, Data Cloud ingestion, and Einstein AI Copilot capabilities.

AGENTFORCE CONVERSATION CREDITS:
- Autonomous Agent Conversations: Priced at $2.00 per conversation across Agentforce Service Agents.
- Conversation Definition: A conversation is defined as an end-to-end customer interaction session resolving an issue or concluding customer intent.
- Minimum Volume: Sold in packages of 10,000 conversations ($20,000 per pack).
