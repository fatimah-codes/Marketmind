TITLE: Press Release: Global B2B Customer Support AI Spending Surges to $4.8B
VENDOR: MarketWire
SOURCE TYPE: press_release
PUBLISHED: 2026-02-05
TARGET ENTITY: Market Overview

MARKET SIZING AND SPEND PROJECTIONS:
SAN FRANCISCO, CA — Enterprise spending on AI-powered customer service and contact center automation reached $4.8 billion in 2025, up 34% year-over-year from $3.58 billion in 2024.

KEY PROJECTIONS:
- By 2028, total market spend on AI customer support platforms is projected by IDC to reach $9.6 billion, representing a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 26.0%.
- Mid-market companies (100–1,000 employees) represent the fastest-growing segment, expanding their AI support investments by 48% annually.
- Hybrid pricing models combining agent seat licensing with consumption/resolution charges now represent 58% of new vendor contracts signed in 2025.
