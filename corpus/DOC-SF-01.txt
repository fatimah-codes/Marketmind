ARCHITECTURE & EINSTEIN 1 PLATFORM:
Salesforce Service Cloud integrates AI through Einstein 1 Platform and Agentforce Service Agents:
- Einstein Trust Layer: Enterprise security layer enforcing zero data retention with LLM vendors, dynamic masking of PII (personally identifiable information), toxicity detection, and audit logging of generated text.
- Agentforce Autonomous Agents: Autonomous reasoning agents capable of performing complex transactional multi-step workflows (issuing refunds, updating shipping addresses, rebooking orders) directly within Salesforce Data Cloud.
- Service Copilot: Provides real-time conversation summarization, next best actions, and automated email response drafting.
